# Chrome Web Store listing, English (MH-484)

**Name:** Moharrik

**Summary (132 characters max):**
Run your Moharrik workspace's approved browser tasks in this browser, only on the sites your workspace allows.

**Category:** Productivity (Workflow & Planning)

**Language:** English

## Description

Moharrik runs the browser tasks your business set up, in a browser you choose, on the sites you
allow, and nowhere else.

Your team defines a task on the Moharrik site, such as collecting a page of search results,
filling in a form or publishing a post it has approved. This extension carries the task out in its
own tab, with real clicks and typing, and reports every step back to your workspace, where you can
see what happened.

**What it does**
- Connects to your workspace in one click: press "Connect to the site" and approve the browser on
  the Moharrik site. Nothing is copied or typed between screens.
- Runs each task in its own tab in a separate "Moharrik" window, so your own tabs are left alone.
- Records what you do in a tab and turns it into a task you can review, schedule and run again.
- Pauses with one switch, and "Stop all automation" halts every browser in the workspace.

**What keeps it safe**
- It only goes to the sites your workspace lists for this browser. The Moharrik server and the
  extension both check every step against that list, and a task that strays from it is stopped at
  once.
- An empty list allows nothing.
- An admin can disconnect the browser from the Moharrik site at any time, and its access ends
  immediately.
- While a task runs, Chrome shows its debugging banner on that tab, so you can always see when
  Moharrik is working.

Moharrik needs a Moharrik account: https://app.moharrik.com
